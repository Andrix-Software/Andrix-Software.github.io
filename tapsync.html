Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

TapSync ("we", "our", or "the app") is a timing and rhythm game for Android developed by Ashwin S Ashok. TapSync is intended for adults aged 18 and older. This privacy policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

The adult-only access and advertising controls described below apply to the updated TapSync release that includes the age-selection screen. Older installed versions may not include these controls. Please update the app when the release becomes available.

Information We Collect

Game Data

TapSync stores game progress, scores, statistics, purchase-entitlement status, and preferences on your device. Android may back up these preferences or transfer them to another device according to your device settings. Your selected age group is excluded from these backups and transfers.

We do not operate our own game-data server. Scores and achievements may be sent to Google Play Games when you are signed in, and the services described below process their own data.

Advertising

TapSync asks for an age group before requesting ads. The choice is stored on this device and excluded from app backup; no date of birth is requested. You can update it in Settings > Age preferences.

Users selecting under 18 or “Prefer not to say” cannot access gameplay in the updated release, and the app does not request ads for them. The updated release does not display banner ads. Users selecting 18 or older may receive G-rated Google AdMob rewarded and interstitial ads after the applicable privacy checks. AdMob may process device information, IP address and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads. The app does not request the Google Advertising ID permission. Where required, privacy choices are collected through Google's consent form and can be revisited in Settings > Ad privacy choices.

Purchase Information

TapSync offers optional in-app purchases (tip jar and, for eligible adult users where offered, remove ads) through Google Play Billing. All payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment details such as credit card numbers or billing addresses.

Google Play Games Services

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the app may access:

  • Your Play Games player ID and display name
  • Leaderboard scores and achievements

This data is managed by Google Play Games Services in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Core gameplay does not require Play Games sign-in. In the updated release, Play Games is initialized only when a user who selected 18 or older chooses a Play Games feature. The app blocks its Play Games calls for under-18 and unknown-age users. You can manage your Play Games account and records through Google Play Games settings.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

The updated release does not include Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. It does not send app usage analytics or crash reports through those services. Older releases included these services; data previously processed by Google remains subject to Firebase's privacy and retention practices.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • The app does not ask you to enter your name, email address, or phone number to play. Play Games may provide a display name, and contacting support voluntarily shares your email address and message with us.
  • Precise location data (GPS)
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Browsing history
  • Microphone or camera data

Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — for age-appropriate advertising (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Billing — for processing in-app purchases (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Games Services — for leaderboards and achievements (Google Privacy Policy)

Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Local progress and preferences remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android backups may restore eligible data after reinstalling. Clearing local storage or uninstalling does not automatically delete backup copies, Google Play Games records, purchases, or data already processed by other services.

Google processes and retains service data according to the linked policies and the applicable service settings. You can manage Android backups and Google Play Games data through the corresponding Google or device settings. Contact us at the address below for privacy questions or requests; we may need information to identify the relevant data and may direct you to the service that controls it.

We do not operate our own game-data servers. Google services may process data outside your country under their published privacy and security practices.

Children's Privacy

TapSync is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not directed at children. The updated release uses a self-declared age group to restrict gameplay and advertising access to users selecting 18 or older. The age screen is not identity or age verification. An age group is stored locally; it is not sent as an analytics event. The updated release does not include Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics and blocks app-initiated Play Games features for users under 18 or whose age is unknown. Parents or guardians can contact us with questions about a child's use of the app or associated data.

Your Choices

  • Opt out of personalized ads: Use Settings > Ad privacy choices when available
  • Remove ads: Eligible adult users can purchase the "Remove Ads" option when ads are enabled
  • Play Games sign-in: Signing in is optional; you can disconnect in Settings > Google > Play Games
  • Age preferences: Update your age-group choice in Settings > Age preferences. No date of birth is requested.
  • Uninstall: Clear app storage or uninstall to remove local data. Backups and third-party records are managed separately as described above

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent for material changes.
