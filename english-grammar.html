English Grammar Learn privacy policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026. This policy covers English Grammar Learn version 2.1 and later, package com.andrix.englishgrammar, published by the developer using the GitHub identity ASHWIN-S-ASHOK.

Offline learning and local storage

Lessons, practice examples and quizzes are bundled with the app. Bookmarks, completed lessons, reading preferences, last lesson and quiz best score are stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not operate its own server for this information. Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes local progress.

Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob banner and optional interstitial advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform for consent and privacy choices. These services use network access. Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can infer approximate location), device or other identifiers, advertising interactions and diagnostic/performance information. Google processes this information for advertising, measurement, security, fraud prevention and other purposes described in its policies. The app does not intentionally send your lesson text, bookmarks or quiz answers to Google.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures for current details. Google controls its processing and retention.

Consent and choices

Where required, Google's consent form is requested before advertisements load. Use the app's Ad privacy control to read a summary and reopen available Google privacy choices. Available options depend on region and Google's consent requirements. Your Android device settings also provide advertising identifier and privacy controls. Learning content remains available offline and is not conditional on a purchase.

Older versions

The legacy version 1 included AppBrain. AppBrain is removed from this upgraded app. Disclosures for any older version still distributed must cover that version separately.

Purchases

This version does not include in-app purchases or subscriptions and does not collect payment details.

Contact and changes

For questions or deletion requests, contact the developer through the support email displayed on this app's Google Play listing. Locally stored progress can be deleted directly by clearing app storage. This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change.
